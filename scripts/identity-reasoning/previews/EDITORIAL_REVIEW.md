# Identity Reasoning Engine v1 — Editorial Review

These are development-only review artifacts. They are not wired to the app, do
not replace any production identity, and should not be bulk-applied.

## Review outputs

- `aries-rat.editorial-review.json`: seven-section Aries × Rat identity
- `pisces-horse.editorial-review.json`: seven-section Pisces × Horse identity
- `../fixtures/libra-snake.editorial-benchmark.json`: frozen approved benchmark

## Self-critique

### Aries × Rat

- **Observational depth:** Strongest in the distinction between fast action and
  fast strategic processing. The rescue/ownership loop gives the shadow a
  believable cost instead of turning speed into uncomplicated praise.
- **Behavioral specificity:** Concrete behaviors recur without being cloned:
  calling a supplier, taking over an orphaned task, noticing reciprocal effort,
  and using a small completion to regain direction.
- **Synthesis:** Aries supplies initiation and directness; Rat supplies
  scanning, leverage, social memory, and a backup route. The emergent person is
  an opportunistic first mover, not a list of bold and clever traits.
- **Coherence and uniqueness:** Reciprocity, selective momentum, and the
  difference between initiation and ownership link every section. Each section
  advances a different consequence of those themes.
- **Rhythm:** Short declarative turns and active verbs create a more immediate
  pace than Libra × Snake. It does not inherit the benchmark's slow-certainty
  structure.
- **Love and Work:** Both derive from the same need for active participation.
  Love asks whether effort comes from both directions; Work asks whether a task
  can move without this person's adrenaline.
- **Editorial risk:** “Useful momentum” is compelling but could become a generic
  high-agency theme if reused broadly. Future edits should protect the Rat-like
  attention to leverage and reciprocity, not merely intensify Aries speed.

### Pisces × Horse

- **Observational depth:** Strongest in the distinction between freedom and
  flight. Emotional permeability affects capacity, while movement helps this
  person separate their feelings from the atmosphere around them.
- **Behavioral specificity:** The changing table, long drive, avoided message,
  borrowed object, shared song, and quiet invitation make receptivity visible in
  ordinary life.
- **Synthesis:** Pisces supplies atmosphere, resonance, and porous attention;
  Horse supplies motion, autonomy, and resistance to confinement. The emergent
  person seeks “belonging with doors,” rather than alternating between two trait
  lists.
- **Coherence and uniqueness:** Spacious commitment, chosen return, and form as
  a container recur across restoration, love, and work without repeating the
  same conclusion.
- **Rhythm:** Longer, more associative sentences and sensory transitions make
  this identity materially softer and more roaming than Aries × Rat or Libra ×
  Snake.
- **Love and Work:** Both translate the same paradox. Love needs closeness that
  does not monitor; Work needs enough form for a private vision to become
  usable.
- **Editorial risk:** Language such as “resonance,” “shoreline,” and “inner
  weather” approaches the poetic ceiling. A later tightening pass could replace
  one or two metaphors with plainer observed moments while preserving the
  identity's associative rhythm.

## Benchmark comparison

Neither test identity is Libra × Snake in different clothing. Aries × Rat is
organized around rapid leverage, reciprocal initiative, and selective action.
Pisces × Horse is organized around emotional atmosphere, movement, and spacious
commitment. The benchmark's signature concern—waiting until judgment earns
trust—is not used as either identity's central engine.

The benchmark remains slightly stronger at effortless compression: several of
its observations feel inevitable in fewer words. The test identities match its
behavioral standard and cross-section logic, but both can still benefit from an
editorial reduction pass after human review. That is the correct stopping point
for v1; no production copy should change before that review.
