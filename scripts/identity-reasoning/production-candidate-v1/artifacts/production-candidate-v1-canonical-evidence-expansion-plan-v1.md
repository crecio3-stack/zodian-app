# Production Candidate v1 — Canonical Evidence Expansion Plan v1

Status: planning only. This document does not add, rewrite, or approve canonical evidence; it does not change the writer, applicability policy, completeness gate, or frozen cohort.

## Goal

Expand stable, citation-ready behavioral evidence where the coverage audit shows that the canonical model cannot support a situation-level Today’s Lens. New evidence must describe a repeatable human pattern that may apply across multiple ordinary situations. It must not be reverse-engineered to rescue one frozen matrix case.

## Required evidence bundle

For each identity/arena evidence expansion, collect only independently supportable items:

| Evidence class | Citation-ready proposition shape |
|---|---|
| Situation trigger | `When [ordinary recurring condition] is present, [concrete context] becomes relevant.` |
| Observable first behavior | `You [visible verb] [ordinary object, exchange, or decision].` |
| Observable second behavior or consequence | `You [different visible behavior]` or `[Practical/interpersonal result] follows.` |
| Interpersonal or practical effect | `This leaves [observable coordination, timing, ownership, or response] unresolved/clear.` |
| Tension or contrast | `You [first observable behavior], but [separate observable behavior].` |
| Grounded action pattern | `You [specific ordinary action] before/when [recurring condition].` |

These are proposition shapes, not copy to add. A bundle is not complete until each claim has its own exact canonical citation and does not rely on motive, diagnosis, generic advice, or a scenario-only consequence.

## Authoring and acceptance rules

1. Start from a canonical identity’s stable behavioral record, not a target Lens sentence.
2. Require at least two independently cited evidence classes before claiming a new complete situation bundle.
3. Keep one citation per proposition. Do not combine weak claims into a synthetic second beat.
4. Test each new proposition against more than one ordinary situation family before accepting it as canonical.
5. Send any new primary insight through Enumeration → Applicability → Human Selection → Person-First/Human-Language/Approval → Completeness. No new evidence goes directly to the writer.
6. Re-run the coverage audit only after evidence is authored and independently reviewed.
