# Deferred deterministic scenario scheduling

No scheduler is implemented. Before automatic shadow coverage can be considered,
a separate approved design must define a versioned calendar-to-scenario
schedule, its start date, timezone, transition behavior, backfill policy,
replacement policy, and longitudinal repetition audit. It must be explicit
rather than positional, modulo-based, inferred from production copy, or derived
from the current production activation-theme rotation.
