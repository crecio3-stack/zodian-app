# Canonical Lens v3.1 blind evaluation

## Caveat

The reveal mappings became visible through the uploaded JSON preview. This review is not
treated as perfectly uncontaminated. The reviewer evaluated the Markdown pairs on
editorial quality rather than intentionally selecting by version label. The margin is
large enough to treat the outcome as directionally decisive.

## Result

- Pairs reviewed: 36
- V2 preferred: 35
- V3.1 preferred: 1
- Ties: 0

Do not run the full 1,440-output v3.1 corpus.

## Interpretation

V3.1 solved acceptance, retry preservation, leakage, and corpus repetition. It regressed
surface voice. Titles often resembled worksheet or planning labels, and prose exposed the
canonical reasoning scaffold instead of turning it into recognizable behavior. V2 more
often produced the stronger `how did it know that?` moment by showing the room, exchange,
pause, or repeated action.

The canonical specificity remains useful. Strong v3.1 details appeared in Sagittarius ×
Monkey opportunity, Aries × Rat confidence, Pisces × Rooster confidence, Gemini × Tiger
work, and Aquarius × Rat confidence. The defect is editorial translation, not fixture or
manifestation quality.

## Decision

Keep the corpus controls, content-word root counting, patch-only retries, deterministic
field preservation, and 180-output validation batch. Revise only the model-writing
surface voice and blind-artifact packaging.
