# One-Paragraph Writer v4

The v3 evidence separation remains unchanged. The writer receives no raw reasoning evidence.

## Narrow v4 addition

The writer receives a clean `plain_insight` plus an optional, separately evidence-approved `plain_action`. The action may complete a read without making the writer invent advice.

- Do not restate the insight in another sentence.
- Two sentences are acceptable; do not add filler to reach a target length.
- A title must describe the pattern or useful direction, never endorse the unwanted behavior.
- If no action is supplied, do not invent one.

No new reasoning system, cleanup stage, or six-field output is introduced.
