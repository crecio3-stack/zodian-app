# Zodian Brand Voice Guide

This document is the editorial source of truth for Zodian.

Use it for product copy, AI prompts, notifications, onboarding, website updates, App Store copy, feature naming, empty states, paywalls, share text, and support language.

This is not marketing copy. It is the standard for how Zodian sounds.

The goal is simple: Zodian should feel like one product, designed by one team, over many years.

## Brand Positioning

Astrology is the lens.

Zodian brings you into focus.

See the patterns that have been there all along.

Astrology is the mechanism.

Self-understanding is the outcome.

Patterns are the bridge.

## What Zodian Is

Zodian helps people recognize recurring patterns in themselves and the people around them.

It is not trying to predict the future.

It is not assigning fixed labels.

It is not scoring relationships.

It helps people notice what has already been there.

## What Zodian Is Not

Zodian is not a horoscope app.

Zodian is not a compatibility calculator.

Zodian is not a personality quiz.

Zodian is not a productivity app with astrology branding.

Zodian is not therapy or coaching.

Zodian does not predict outcomes.

Zodian helps people notice recurring patterns through the lens of astrology.

## Core Principles

Every sentence should move toward at least one of these ideas:

- noticing
- perspective
- reflection
- recurring patterns
- self-understanding
- understanding others
- clarity
- focus
- curiosity
- growth through observation

## Voice

Zodian should feel:

- calm
- confident
- premium
- warm
- reflective
- intelligent
- emotionally mature
- observational
- modern
- concise

Zodian should never feel:

- dramatic
- mystical
- salesy
- preachy
- generic
- diagnostic
- mechanical
- score-oriented

## Emotional Journey

Every interaction with Zodian should move the user gently through four emotional states:

1. Curiosity

   > I wonder if this will describe me.

2. Recognition

   > That actually feels familiar.

3. Understanding

   > I have never thought about myself that way before.

4. Reflection

   > I will probably notice that today.

Every feature should support at least one of these emotional moments.

Recognition is the most important emotional moment in Zodian. Every feature should strive to create the feeling:

> I have never noticed that before, but it feels true.

The goal is not simply to deliver information. The goal is to create recognition.

## The Zodian Formula

Whenever possible, product copy naturally follows this progression:

1. Perspective
2. Pattern
3. Understanding
4. Growth

The copy should not skip directly to conclusions.

Do not tell people who they are. Help them notice what keeps showing up.

## The Zodian Test

When designing a new feature or writing new copy, ask:

Does this help someone notice something about themselves?

Does this help someone understand another person?

Does this create recognition instead of prediction?

Would this still feel valuable if astrology were invisible?

If the answer to most of these is no, reconsider the feature or copy.

## Show, Do Not Explain

Whenever possible, show the experience instead of describing the experience.

Instead of:

> Today’s Lens provides personalized insights.

Prefer:

> A concrete Today’s Lens example that feels specific and quietly true.

Instead of:

> Pattern Memory tracks recurring themes.

Prefer:

> A Pattern Memory example showing something quietly returning over time.

The product should create recognition before explanation.

- reflection

- curiosity
- recurring

- coming into focus

- quietly
- meaningful

## Vocabulary to Avoid

Avoid product language like:

- compatibility score
- percentage match
- personality test
- prediction
- fortune
- destiny
- unlock your potential
- life-changing
- magic
- algorithm
- AI-powered
- engagement
- insights engine
- power user

Avoid copy that sounds like a generic astrology app, a dating app, a productivity app, or a personality quiz.

## Product Writing

UI copy should be:

- short
- clear
- human
- specific
- emotionally grounded

Never write a paragraph where one sentence will do.

Prefer quiet clarity over cleverness.

Prefer observation over explanation.

Prefer concrete behavior over abstract traits.

## Marketing Writing

Marketing should create curiosity.

It should not explain everything.

The goal is to make someone think:

> I need to see mine.

Not:

> I understand how the product works.

Marketing should invite recognition, not over-teach the system.

## AI Writing

Generated content should feel like observation.

Not diagnosis.

Not certainty.

Not prediction.

Avoid:

> You are...

Prefer:

> You may notice...

> A pattern that often appears...

> It may become clearer...

> What keeps returning...

The AI should help users observe themselves, not define themselves.

AI copy should still feel authored by Zodian:

- specific, not vague
- grounded, not mystical
- emotionally clear, not therapeutic
- reflective, not explanatory
- memorable, not dramatic

## Connect

Connect is not about compatibility.

It is about understanding.

Every relationship reveals different patterns.

The emotional payoff is:

> I understand this person better.

Not:

> We scored highly together.

Connect copy should avoid scores, percentages, ranked matches, and language that makes people feel evaluated.

Use Connect to help people notice what feels familiar, different, interesting, or worth returning to.

## Pattern Memory

Pattern Memory is one of Zodian's signature experiences.

It should always feel reflective.

It notices what keeps returning over time.

It should never feel like analytics, reporting, or a dashboard.

Pattern Memory copy should feel like a quiet realization:

> This keeps showing up.

Not:

> Here is your activity summary.

## Today’s Lens

Today’s Lens should feel:

- timely
- specific
- personal
- grounded
- quietly memorable

Readers should think:

> That felt surprisingly true.

Not:

> That predicted my day.

Today’s Lens should frame the day as a lens, not a forecast.

## Onboarding

Onboarding should make the user curious about themselves.

It should not over-explain astrology.

It should not feel like a quiz.

The best onboarding copy helps the user feel:

> There is something about me I am about to see more clearly.

## Notifications

Notifications should feel like a quiet invitation back to perspective.

Avoid generic reminder copy.

Avoid urgency for its own sake.

Avoid prediction-coded language.

Prefer:

- Something is coming into focus.
- Today may make a familiar feeling clearer.
- Notice what keeps returning.
- A different part of your pattern is showing today.

## Premium and Rewards

Premium copy should not feel transactional or inflated.

Rewards should feel like access to deeper reflection, not gamification.

Avoid:

- power user language
- aggressive upgrade language
- exaggerated benefit claims
- "unlock your potential" framing

Prefer:

- revisit what keeps returning
- see the larger pattern
- return to what became meaningful
- open a deeper look when you want it

## Share Copy

Share copy should feel like an artifact someone would actually want to send.

It should be concise, distinctive, and self-contained.

Avoid explaining the product in share text.

Prefer a line that makes someone curious enough to ask:

> What is yours?

## App Store and Website Copy

Public-facing copy should keep the same worldview:

Astrology offers perspective.

Patterns become visible.

Understanding grows from what keeps returning.

The website and App Store listing should not sound broader, louder, or more generic than the app.

Do not over-explain the mechanics.

Create curiosity first.

## Brand Filters

Before shipping any new copy, ask:

Could this sentence appear in any astrology app?

If yes, rewrite it.

Does this sentence create curiosity?

If not, improve it.

Does this sentence sound like prediction?

If yes, soften it.

Does this sentence help someone notice something?

If not, reconsider it.

Does this sound unmistakably like Zodian?

If not, continue refining.

## Examples

Weak:

> Your compatibility score is 91%.

Better:

> Some people immediately feel familiar.
>
> Zodian helps you understand why.

Weak:

> Discover your personality.

Better:

> Notice the patterns that shape how you move through the world.

Weak:

> Unlock hidden insights.

Better:

> See what has been there all along.

Weak:

> Your horoscope for today.

Better:

> Today's pattern.

Weak:

> This connection activates growth.

Better:

> Some people show you what you keep circling back to.

Weak:

> AI-powered astrology insights.

Better:

> Astrology as a lens for what keeps coming into focus.

## Simplicity

The best Zodian writing rarely tries to impress.

It creates one clear thought.

One memorable observation.

One feeling worth returning to.

If a sentence can be shorter without losing meaning, shorten it.

If an explanation can become an experience, show it instead.

Quiet confidence is stronger than cleverness.

## Future Development Rule

Before implementing any new feature, onboarding flow, notification, marketing campaign, AI prompt, website copy, or App Store change:

Review this document.

Before making any user-facing copy changes, review `BRAND_VOICE.md` and ensure all new writing follows its principles.

If there is a conflict between older copy and this guide, this guide is the source of truth.

New writing should extend the existing voice, not reinvent it.

When uncertain, choose language that helps people observe rather than language that claims certainty.

The objective is that every future interaction, inside the app, on the website, in marketing, and in AI-generated content, feels unmistakably like Zodian.
