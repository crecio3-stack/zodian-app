## Description

Zodian is a ritual-first astrology app for people who want more than a generic horoscope.

Start with onboarding that blends Western and Chinese zodiac signals into a personalized identity reveal. From there, return each day for a fresh reading, complete a short ritual to build streaks and rewards, and unlock a deeper Blueprint that explores your emotional patterns, strengths, shadows, and compatibility style.

Zodian also introduces a more intentional way to explore connection. In Connect, you can browse curated compatibility profiles, save standout matches, revisit compatibility details, and continue the conversation inside a lightweight in-app chat experience.

Inside Zodian you can:
- Reveal a daily reading with love, work, growth, and caution themes
- Complete a short daily ritual and track streak-based rewards
- Explore your Blueprint with deeper archetype and compatibility insight
- Save standout connections and revisit why they resonated
- Edit your profile and shape how you appear inside Connect

Zodian is designed to feel reflective, cinematic, and easy to return to every day.
