# Canonical Identity Distillation Pilot v1 — Review

## Libra × Snake

- Defining contradiction: warm social care and tactful influence versus private comfort, admiration, and indirect control.
- Strongest motivation: influence through rapport rather than force.
- Strength: turns difficult differences into calm appeals.
- Friction: comfort and indulgence can compete with follow-through.
- Blind spot: charm can obscure when a firmer boundary is needed.
- Interpersonal pattern: builds loyalty through warmth, tact, and visible care.
- Emotional pattern: feels pulled between caring for others and protecting personal comfort.
- Distinctness: guides through warmth and social care, unlike Sagittarius × Monkey’s strategic leverage.
- Changed notes: replaced abstract motivation and emotional language with comfort, admiration, and care-versus-comfort tension.

## Taurus × Horse

- Defining contradiction: strong ability and high self-regard versus the ordinary effort required to earn trust.
- Strongest motivation: visible proof that ability has weight.
- Strength: brings discipline and high standards to a chosen craft.
- Friction: confidence in talent can collide with the slow work of earning trust.
- Blind spot: may mistake being overlooked for being undervalued.
- Interpersonal pattern: keeps private matters guarded while caring about public regard.
- Emotional pattern: can become withdrawn or irritable when feeling misunderstood.
- Distinctness: combines craft discipline with status sensitivity and reluctance toward ordinary social effort.
- Changed notes: simplified two phrases without changing the profile’s source-supported structure.

## Sagittarius × Monkey

- Defining contradiction: broad aims and leadership versus strategic self-interest, emotional reserve, and resistance to obligation.
- Strongest motivation: influence that advances a large purpose.
- Strength: organizes large aims into clear action.
- Friction: public ambition can conflict with emotional closeness.
- Blind spot: may hide self-interest behind a larger mission.
- Interpersonal pattern: uses timing, competence, and persuasion to build authority.
- Emotional pattern: keeps emotional obligations secondary to larger aims.
- Distinctness: gains influence through strategy, timing, leverage, and controlled presentation rather than Libra × Snake’s warmth.
- Changed notes: restored the source-supported tension between larger ideals and calculated self-interest; removed corporate and therapeutic phrasing.

## Gemini × Dragon

- Defining contradiction: outward confidence and fast social energy versus intense loyalty needs and easy emotional wounding.
- Strongest motivation: momentum, attention, and a new possibility.
- Strength: generates contagious ideas and gathers people around them.
- Friction: novelty can conflict with sustained work.
- Blind spot: may read ordinary distance as a loss of loyalty.
- Interpersonal pattern: uses presence, humor, and confidence to draw attention.
- Emotional pattern: a commanding front can hide a more easily wounded core.
- Distinctness: combines attention-seeking social force with a particularly intense reaction to rejection or lost attention.
- Changed notes: replaced an inferred recovery pattern with the source-supported confident-front/vulnerable-core tension.
