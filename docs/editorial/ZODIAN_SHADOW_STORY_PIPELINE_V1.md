# Zodian Shadow Story Pipeline v1

The shadow pipeline tests a provider-free transformation from stable canonical identity material to one temporary, useful editorial thread. Its output is an internal Story Engine brief, never reader-facing prose.

Identity remains stable. A scenario selects exactly one canonical profile signal and supplies a manually authored temporary pressure plus internal directions. The resulting brief must not summarize the full identity, predict an event, fix a scene, copy source language, or give advice.

The eight scenarios are deterministic fixtures, not claims about a real calendar day or astronomical conditions. They test whether one identity can yield more than one recognizable mini story while leaving the reader to supply people, setting, decision, and circumstance.

The pipeline is isolated and provider-free. It performs no writing, model invocation, source ingestion, database work, publication, routing, scheduling, or production integration.

## Brief direction standard

Each brief holds one live tension open for a future writer to explore. The perspective shift changes the frame without supplying a lesson or recommendation. The landing direction points to what has become visible without prescribing an action; it must not use task-shaped language such as “the useful move,” “the next step,” “one clear contribution,” “the next choice,” “should,” or “can show.”
