## libra-snake-boundary

- Identity/signal: Libra × Snake; `commonBlindSpots` — Charm can obscure when a firmer boundary is needed.
- Pressure: A small discomfort keeps getting softened so the real limit stays unnamed.
- Brief: daily thread — A softened discomfort is asking for a clear limit. Central tension — Keeping the mood easy versus naming what no longer works. Question — What changes once the issue is named without managing every reaction? Hook — The problem is not as small as it has been made to seem. Shift — A boundary can be direct without becoming harsh. Landing — The next honest limit matters more than a perfect response.
- Fit/difference: turns charm into a boundary story, unlike the second Libra × Snake scenario’s care-and-control story.
- Writer opportunity/risk: make the limit recognizable without inventing a scene or becoming advice prose.
