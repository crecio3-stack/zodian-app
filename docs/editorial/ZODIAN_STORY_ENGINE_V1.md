# Zodian Story Engine v1

## Product purpose

A Zodian read identifies the part of the reader’s day most worth paying attention to. It should feel relevant enough that the reader trusts the read, remembers it when something happens, and returns tomorrow for guidance.

## The creative unit

The engine identifies one daily thread: one tension, one question, one emotional movement, and one perspective shift. It is not the reader’s whole day, their whole personality, a detailed invented event, a list of themes, or generic advice. It is one mini story within a larger picture.

## Reader participation

The thread must be specific enough to feel relevant but open enough for the reader to supply who, where, the exact conversation, the exact decision, and the exact circumstance. Prefer a clear emotional situation over a fixed external scene.

## Hook

The first sentence of a future read must create immediate curiosity. It should open a question in the reader’s mind and make them continue. The hook may be a warning, a question, a strong observation, a contradiction, or a choice. It must not be generic horoscope warm-up language.

## Narrative movement

A future read moves through a hook, one relevant daily thread, complication or tension, perspective shift, and a clear landing. This guides emotional movement; it is not a rigid sentence template.

## Trust goal

The reader should finish feeling: this applies to something real in my day; this gave me useful perspective; and I may remember this later when the situation happens.

## Voice

Clear, concise, natural, and understandable immediately. Avoid vague abstractions and decorative language.

## v1 boundary

Story Engine v1 produces an internal editorial brief only. It is isolated and shadow-only: it does not generate reader-facing copy and has no connection to the current writer, generation, validation, batching, publication, resolver, scheduled routing, provider model, database, deployment, or cron job 26.

## Validation boundary

The validator is a light deterministic tripwire. It catches plainly empty, technical, command-like, fixed-scene, competing-thread, overly long, or horoscope-prose briefs. It is not intended to classify every possible invented scene or competing theme. Semantic single-thread selection belongs to a future controlled Story Engine generation and selection layer.
