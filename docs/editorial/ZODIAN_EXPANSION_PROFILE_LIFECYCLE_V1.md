# Zodian Expansion Profile Lifecycle v1

`source_ready` has no profile or review outcome. `editorial_review` has a complete valid profile and no final approval. `approved` has a valid profile plus source-support, field, contradiction, difficult-trait, blind-label, Story Engine, and human approval evidence. `blocked` has no profile and documents the source limitation.

Allowed transitions are source-ready to editorial-review or blocked; editorial-review to itself, approved with full evidence, or blocked with a genuine source gap. Approved and blocked states do not regress. This standalone contract is not integrated into Expansion Cohort 24; Wave B authoring remains unauthorized until that integration milestone.
